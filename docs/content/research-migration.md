# Research content migration

[简体中文](research-migration.zh-CN.md)

The reading guide connects the original fixed report to the current investigations. The complete English and Chinese v1.0.10 Markdown files are retained byte-for-byte under `public/downloads/`. The editions are wording snapshots dated November 1, 2026; their evidence dates vary and linked sites remain external. Source hashes are registered as SRC-ARCHIVE and SRC-ARCHIVE-EN.

The historical index contains 35 H research themes and seven R evidence-recovery records. These are not 42 independent experiments. Titles, historical scope, findings, open issues and original line references are projected into `src/data/reading-guide.json`. The complete source remains downloadable. No archived statement is silently upgraded to the current evidence stage.

Three chapter-local instruments use existing reviewed sources. Conditional means and intervals reuse the atlas conditioning projection; exclusions reuse the atlas exclusion projection. The seven window rows are extracted from the exact fixed-report table and retain distinct original, rerun and 45-observation identities. Metrics and sample definitions are not pooled. Each instrument has an exact table and immutable source link.

The expansion reorganizes reviewed public work. It does not rerun historical strategies, upgrade reading/claim stages or publish private BMLL observations. Site/source tests verify archive hashes, historical identities and exact window values. Browser checks cover chart controls, language changes, downloads, history search and mobile containment.
