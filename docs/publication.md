# Publication boundary

This public project contains original explanatory writing, citation metadata, research status and reviewed aggregate evidence. It contains no raw vendor records, credentials, local machine paths, private signals, portfolio weights or full-text paper mirrors.

The BMLL pilot is an exploratory research input outside the first public release. Do not copy its security-level samples or claim a full L3 feed from a reference label. Public snippets already present in upstream records do not grant blanket permission to publish new provider outputs.

Review date, source data cutoff and historical experiment date are distinct. Historical rule returns, factor associations, synthetic-share ledgers, raw-share accounting and actual executions remain separate objects. A successful query is not a settled corporate action; a balanced ledger is not calibrated market impact.

Literature stages: `abstract`, `author_summary`, `fulltext`, `replicated`. Cross-check stages: `untested`, `not_comparable`, `supports`, `conflicts`. Scope and version must accompany a stronger claim. Our first five papers are not full-text reviewed or replicated, and their cross-checks remain untested.

The original fixed report and source snapshots retain their exact identities and rights notices. Public hosting does not grant rights to redistribute a paper or provider dataset. No additional blanket license is selected on the owner's behalf.
